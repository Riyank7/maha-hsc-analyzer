MAHARASHTRA HSC ANALYZER

Privacy and sign-in

This website lets you browse public Maharashtra HSC examination papers and their marks analysis.

Google identity

When you sign in, the app requests your Google account identifier, verified email address, and display name. It uses these details to authenticate you and show your signed-in account. It does not request access to Gmail, Drive, contacts, or other Google files.

Your session

The app stores your identity in an HttpOnly, signed browser cookie for up to eight hours. Signing out clears that browser cookie. The app does not maintain an account database or retain Google access or refresh tokens. Display preferences, such as the selected subject and year, may be held temporarily in browser session storage.

Hosting and service providers

Google provides authentication, Cloudflare hosts and serves the website, and GitHub hosts its source and deployment workflow. These services may process connection details and operational logs under their own privacy policies. Google identity credentials are exchanged with the app server over HTTPS; client secrets are stored encrypted in Cloudflare.

Public examination material

The examination papers and research data are public educational material. Google sign-in controls access through this website; the public GitHub repository also contains examination PDFs.

Contact

For questions about sign-in or privacy, contact riyank.deep@gmail.com. You can also remove the app’s authorization from your Google account permissions.

Locked Answers

The Answers section also uses a shared password. Its salted verifier and a separate answer decryption key are stored as encrypted Cloudflare secrets. A successful unlock sets an additional signed HttpOnly cookie for two hours, tied to your signed-in Google account. Locking the section or signing out clears that cookie. Original answer files are encrypted before publication. Published solution links lead to external sites with their own privacy and access rules.
