## Answer coverage

All **111 collected, active PDFs** now have answer coverage in the separately
locked Answers library: **68 published solution-reference sets** and
**43 original worked-answer sets** (1,213 grouped question/subquestion records).
There are no remaining preparation or partial-set statuses in this collected
corpus. This does not imply that the missing historical papers have been found.

Original answers include printed alternatives, worked calculations, programs,
and model English writing. Scan ambiguities, defective options and printing
errors are identified beside the relevant answers. Diagram questions currently
include construction/drawing guidance; the library does not promise a rendered
diagram for every such question.

Validation included 79 symbolic Mathematics checks, 20 compiled C++ solutions
with 37 output/edge checks, and 14,933 checks against an 8085 instruction model.
These are targeted checks, not independent teacher marking of every answer or
testing on physical 8085 hardware. Original answers are study solutions, not
official marking schemes. For published references, the first and last linked
answers of each Shaalaa set were checked; every individual publisher answer
has not been independently marked.

The 2009 English retyped copies omit the start of the first passage. Its
home-schooling answer is checked against the original author's account.
Another damaged sentence and the footer-obscured report headline are explained
within that answer set.
